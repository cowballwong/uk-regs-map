# UK Building Regulations Map (England)

An interactive map of the documents that control building work in England and the links between them.

Live: https://anzonwong.com/uk-regs-map/

Always rely on legislation.gov.uk, GOV.UK and the official documents themselves.

## What it is

One connected network you can rotate in 3D (or see in 2D on a phone), England only:

- Building Act 1984, Building Regulations 2010 (Schedule 1 Parts and regulation 7), Building Safety Act 2022 and the 2023 dutyholder, competence and higher-risk building regulations;
- the Approved Documents A to S and Regulation 7 in force;
- CDM 2015 and HSE L153; the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 and the Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022;
- the British Standards the Approved Documents cite, as **citation-only** nodes: number, title and edition as printed in the Approved Document. No BS text is reproduced.

## Version 2 (November 2026)

- **Full text.** Read and search the full text of the Building Regulations 2010 (England text, from legislation.gov.uk) and all 24 Approved Documents in force (from the GOV.UK PDFs), clause by clause. Each reader carries its own source and Open Government Licence attribution, and gives the PDF page for every block. A quoted link can open its sentence in context. The full text loads only when you open it.
- **Edition check.** Shows which cited British Standard editions have a newer edition or are withdrawn, with the Approved Document's own quote, page and the public source that confirms the status. It is a prompt to check, not a ban: the Approved Document says which edition it refers to.
- **Check my spec.** Paste specification text and see which standards it names, how they compare with the editions the Approved Documents print, and download the table as CSV. Runs in your browser only; nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Dark theme** and a search-first start panel.

No British Standard text is reproduced, in the map or in the full text: only numbers, titles and editions as printed in the Approved Documents.

The November 2026 build has 525 nodes, 836 solid links (each one quoted from its source) and 17 dashed links (AI-suggested, labelled as such). Data checked 9 November 2026.

"Newer edition exists" or "withdrawn" is shown only where a public, non-BSI source naming that standard confirms it; that source is cited. Everything else says "not checked".
