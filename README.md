# UK Building Regulations Map (England)

An interactive map of the documents that control building work in England and the links between them.

Live: https://anzonwong.com/uk-regs-map/

> **Disclaimer.** This is a personal project by Anzon Wong (AW Design). It is **not** an official website. It does not represent, and is not endorsed by, the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government, the Building Safety Regulator, the Health and Safety Executive, BSI or any other body. It is a navigation aid, **not legal advice**. Summaries and links may be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Always rely on legislation.gov.uk, GOV.UK and the official documents themselves.

## What it is

One connected network you can rotate in 3D (or see in 2D on a phone), England only:

- Building Act 1984, Building Regulations 2010 (Schedule 1 Parts and regulation 7), Building Safety Act 2022 and the 2023 dutyholder, competence and higher-risk building regulations;
- the Approved Documents A to S and Regulation 7 in force;
- CDM 2015 and HSE L153; the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 and the Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022;
- the British Standards the Approved Documents cite, as **citation-only** nodes: number, title and edition as printed in the Approved Document. No BS text is reproduced.

The November 2026 build has 525 nodes, 836 solid links (each one quoted from its source) and 17 dashed links (AI-suggested, labelled as such). Data checked 9 November 2026.

"Newer edition exists" or "withdrawn" is shown only where a public, non-BSI source naming that standard confirms it; that source is cited. Everything else says "not checked".
